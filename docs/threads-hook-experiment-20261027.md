# Threads opening-line experiment — 2026-10-27

Goal: improve qualified visits and affiliate/note conversions, not likes alone.

## Test design
- Keep posting frequency and destinations unchanged. Never mass-post, fabricate results or promise sales.
- Change **one** variable at a time: the first line of a post.
- A: factual introduction; B: specific reader problem; C: curiosity without withholding essential information.
- Rotate only among comparable posts/topics; avoid simultaneous repeated content.
- Preserve brand voice: Yonaka no Ojisan is gentle, first-person 私; Lens is factual and avoids unsupported medical claims; Rakutabi avoids fabricated prices or availability.
- Record post URL, JST publish time, first line, account, topic, reach, profile visits, link clicks and attributed conversion if available.
- Evaluate after 14 days or adequate sample. If metrics unavailable, mark unknown, not zero. Never infer conversion from likes.
- Before scaling, check Threads platform rules, avoid repetitive promotional content and confirm valid destination pages.

## Examples (drafts only, not authorized live posts)
Lens: 「コンタクト、同じ商品でも買い方で支払額が変わることがあります。」 → existing verified 1day comparison page.
Rakutabi: 「宿を探すとき、写真より先に確認したいことがあります。」 → relevant existing hotel comparison.
Yonaka: 「11月は、頑張ることより先に整えておきたいことがあります。」 → published November note, Sunday CTA policy unchanged.
Pet: 「避難袋に自分の物は入れた。でも、愛犬の物は？」 → primary-source-backed checklist, not unverified product promises.

## Owner decisions
Only request approval for new posting frequency, material editorial direction, account connection or paid distribution. No extra spend.
